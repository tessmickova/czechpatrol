legální cestu, a je v pořádku, když bude odpověď „ne“.

**Subject: Public safety project in the Czech Republic — is there a legal way to show Downdetector signals?**

Dear Downdetector / Ookla team,

CzechPatrol (https://czechpatrol.cz) is an independent, non-commercial
public situational overview for the Czech Republic: it aggregates verified
information about the availability of essential services and about security
incidents, always with the source and time shown. It is not a government
service and does not present itself as one. Operator: [DOPLNIT].

We do not scrape Downdetector and we will not. Today we only link to
downdetector.cz with a short description of what it is. We are writing to
ask whether there is a way, on your terms, to show a **country-level or
region-level indicator** (for example "elevated reports for mobile networks
in the Czech Republic") on a non-commercial public site:

1. Is there a licence, API tier or embeddable widget intended for
   non-commercial or public-interest use? What would it cost?
2. If not, is a plain link with your name the extent of what you allow?
   We will respect that.

We understand that user reports are your product and that the answer may
simply be no. We would rather ask than assume.

Kind regards,
[DOPLNIT]
